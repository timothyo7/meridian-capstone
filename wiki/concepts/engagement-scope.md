# Engagement scope

What the team has been asked to deliver, by when, and against what success
criteria, per the [client brief](../sources/client-brief.md).

## What we know

- Requested artifact: a dashboard of sales performance by store and by
  category ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- Stated success: increase revenue, reduce operating costs, and improve
  customer experience — three goals, unranked ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- Timeline: about eight weeks, with a preliminary readout for the board in
  three weeks ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- Data access is gated on NDA signature, via [Marcus](../entities/marcus.md)
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- The brief describes an artifact and three broad outcomes
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)). *Team inference:* resting on that
  gap, the brief does not state the decision criterion that would make the
  artifact useful, and that gap is worth naming plainly rather than resolving
  by assumption.
