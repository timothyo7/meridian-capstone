# Grocery sector trends

The market Meridian competes in, based on public sources. This page is about
the sector, not about Meridian. Links to Meridian's own position are labeled
as team inference.

## What we know

- U.S. grocery is roughly a $912.4 billion sector, per an IBISWorld estimate
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Share is shifting away from traditional supermarkets. Value-oriented and
  fresh-format concepts are driving growth
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Specialty grocers are gaining share. Trader Joe's led the specialty sector
  with 3% year-over-year growth for the period ending Feb. 28, 6 points ahead
  of the whole sector. The source does not say what the 3% measures
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Traditional grocers such as Publix and Safeway are losing shoppers in every
  income group, and discount grocery growth has plateaued since mid-2025
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Stores are getting smaller. FMI reports U.S. grocery stores are shrinking,
  Gelson's is testing 3,000–5,400 sq ft micromarkets, and Aldi is piloting a
  modular layout for smaller urban footprints
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Grocery is taking dining occasions from restaurants. In a Tillster survey,
  36% of diners said they visit grocery stores more often, while many reported
  visiting fast-food and fast-casual chains less often. This is self-reported,
  and Tillster sells technology to restaurant brands
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- Grocery-anchored shopping centers have low vacancy and command rent premiums.
  Investment in them rose to almost $11 billion in 2025, up 42% from 2024
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- *Team inference:* this rests on Meridian's stated competitive basis of
  prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller footprint
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)). Meridian's positioning matches the
  side of the sector the source describes as growing. The same trends also
  mean larger rivals are moving onto that ground (see
  [Differentiation](differentiation.md)).
