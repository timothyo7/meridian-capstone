# Store performance variation

Why some Meridian stores have outperformed others, per the
[client brief](../sources/client-brief.md).

## What we know

- "Growth has been good, but it hasn't been even" — some stores took off
  immediately, others were slower ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- No definition of "performance" is given ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
  *Team inference:* resting on that silence, revenue, margin, and growth rate
  are all plausible metrics and would lead to different rankings.
- The brief says most of the eight newer stores came from taken-over leases,
  not that all eight did ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)). *Team
  inference:* resting on that distinction, the original six stores and the
  taken-over-lease stores are plausibly different populations and worth
  separating in any analysis.
- Confounders available in the data: square footage, opening date, and lease
  terms ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
