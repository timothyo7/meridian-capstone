# Gelson's

A Southern California grocery chain, and the first regional operator named in
the wiki's public sources.

## What we know

- California-based. It operates 27 traditional grocery stores in Southern
  California ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- It has opened two "ReCharge by Gelson's" micromarkets, billed as "premium
  convenience stores" and paired with Rove EV fast-charging hubs. The first is
  3,000 sq ft in Santa Ana and the second is 5,400 sq ft in Costa Mesa
  ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- The micromarkets sell produce, bread, and beer and wine, plus hot food and
  grab-and-go items ([ICSC, Apr 2026](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md)).
- *Team inference:* this rests on the cities the source names and the brief's
  county list ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)). Santa Ana and Costa Mesa
  are in Orange County, one of Meridian's three counties. The county is the
  team's general knowledge, not something the source states; the article calls
  Costa Mesa part of "the Los Angeles area." Whether Gelson's overlaps with
  Meridian's stores is unknown.
