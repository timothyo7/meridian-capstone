# Pasadena site

The location Dana Okafor names as Meridian's presumed next store, per the
[client brief](../sources/client-brief.md).

## What we know

- Named in the brief as "the obvious next step" for the next location
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- No supporting evidence for that judgment appears anywhere in the brief
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- Commitment status unknown. *Team inference:* the brief says "before we
  commit," which the team reads as implying no commitment yet, but the brief
  does not say so directly ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).
- The brief gives no address or neighborhood for the site
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)).

The Pasadena grocery market (public sources):

- [Sprouts Farmers Market](sprouts.md) already operates a store in
  unincorporated East Pasadena, at Rosemead and Colorado Boulevards ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- As of March 11, 2026, Sprouts was named as the applicant on a
  change-of-ownership notice at a former Rite Aid at 1421 E. Washington Blvd.
  "If approved," it would be Sprouts' first store inside Pasadena city limits.
  The article gives no opening date ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Sprouts has also signed a lease in nearby Highland Park, with opening
  expected in 2027 ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- *Team inference:* this rests on the Sprouts points above. Pasadena is
  attracting specialty grocers, which could be read either way: as proof of
  demand, or as a trade area getting crowded. The source can't settle which,
  and how close Sprouts is to Meridian's site is unknown until the site's
  location is known (Q11, Q14).

## Why this page matters

This entity is the engagement's central assumption, not a conclusion the team
has reached. The wiki's job is to keep the evidence for and against it in one
place, so the assumption gets tested rather than quietly confirmed.
