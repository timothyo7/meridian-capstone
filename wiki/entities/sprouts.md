# Sprouts Farmers Market

A national specialty grocer expanding in the Pasadena area, which is where
Meridian presumes it will open next.

## What we know

- Headquartered in Phoenix. Focused on "fresh produce, natural foods and
  specialty grocery items"
  ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Plans "at least 40 new stores in 2026"
  ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Operates a store in unincorporated East Pasadena at Rosemead and Colorado
  Boulevards ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Is named as the applicant on a change-of-ownership notice at a former Rite
  Aid at 1421 E. Washington Blvd., which would be its first store inside
  Pasadena city limits. The status was pending as of March 11, 2026
  ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Has signed a lease on a former 99 Cents Only Store at 6235 York Blvd.,
  Highland Park, with opening expected in 2027
  ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- Is investing in self-distribution and in customer insight from its loyalty
  program ([Colorado Boulevard, Mar 2026](../sources/sprouts-pasadena-2026.md)).
- *Team inference:* this rests on the Pasadena and Highland Park sites above
  and on Meridian's growth by taking over vacated leases
  ([brief](../sources/client-brief.md)). Sprouts competes with Meridian for
  customers in the Pasadena area and also for the same kind of vacated retail
  space.
