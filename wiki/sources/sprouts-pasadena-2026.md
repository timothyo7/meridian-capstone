# Sprouts Farmers Market expanding to Pasadena and Highland Park — Colorado Boulevard (Rena Kurlander), March 11, 2026

## What it says

A short local news report. A public notice at a former Rite Aid in Pasadena
names Sprouts Farmers Market as the applicant for a change in ownership.
Separately, Sprouts has signed a lease on a former 99 Cents Only Store in
nearby Highland Park. It is the wiki's first source about Pasadena itself.
It reports what a posted notice says, not an announcement from Sprouts, and
nothing in it is about Meridian.

## Key claims

Pasadena:
- A public notice at the former Rite Aid at **1421 E. Washington Blvd.**
  (Washington Boulevard and Hill Avenue) shows "an application for a change in
  ownership." The applicant is SF Markets LLC, doing business as Sprouts
  Farmers Market.
- "If approved," it "would mark the first Sprouts store within Pasadena city
  limits."
- Sprouts already operates a store "nearby" in **unincorporated East
  Pasadena**, at Rosemead Boulevard and Colorado Boulevard.
- **Status is tentative.** The article does not say what kind of application
  the notice is for, give an opening date, or say whether it was approved.

Highland Park:
- A former 99 Cents Only Store at **6235 York Blvd.**, "a few miles away," is
  to become a Sprouts. The lease is signed, and opening is expected in 2027.

Sprouts company:
- Phoenix-based, focused on "fresh produce, natural foods and specialty
  grocery items."
- Plans "at least 40 new stores in 2026," "despite expectations of a
  challenging year ahead."
- Leaders have highlighted investment in self-distribution and "efforts to
  gather deeper customer insights through the chain's loyalty program."

## Questions it raises

- Does leadership know a specialty competitor may be opening inside Pasadena,
  and how does that change "the obvious next step"? That is Q28 in the
  [register](../analysis/open-questions.md). The distance between the Sprouts
  site and Meridian's Pasadena site can't be judged yet, because the brief
  gives no address for the Pasadena site (Q11 and Q14).
- If Sprouts is competing for the same kind of vacated stores Meridian took
  over, the supply of affordable vacated space is tighter. This is local
  evidence for Q25.
- Q22 (specialty competitors in Meridian's counties) is partly informed:
  Sprouts has at least one store in the Pasadena area, with two more planned
  in LA County.
